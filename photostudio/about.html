Our Story

A friend's studio. A broken booking process. A platform that changed everything.

It Started With a Friend and a WhatsApp Group

One of our closest friends, Omar, has owned a photography studio in Zamalek for over five years. He is good at what he does — lighting, space, vibe — and he has built real connections in Cairo's photography and creative scene. Photographers trust him. Studio owners around the city know his name.

But running his studio was a mess. Bookings came in through WhatsApp at midnight. Clients double-confirmed three times over the phone. He kept a paper calendar on his wall that was somehow always wrong. Every week there was a conflict. Every month there was a no-show that cost him money and a time slot he could have filled.

Sitting with Omar one evening — over koshary, because it is always koshary — we started talking about the wider problem. It was not just his studio. Every photography studio in Cairo had the same issue. There was no single place to browse what was available, compare prices, see locations on a map, and book a slot instantly. It was all scattered across Instagram DMs, phone calls, and recommendations from friends.

That is when we said it out loud for the first time: why don't we build a platform that gathers all the studios in Cairo in one place? Omar's connections in the field meant we could actually get studios to join. Our background in software meant we could actually build it properly.

We spent the next few weeks talking to studio owners across Maadi, Nasr City, New Cairo, and Zamalek. The problem was the same everywhere: no digital presence, no way to handle bookings without being physically available, no way to see who their most reliable clients were. Every single owner we spoke to said the same thing when we described the idea: "When can I sign up?"

We built the first version of LensSpace in a focused sprint — a clean, fast platform where any photographer can browse studios across Cairo, filter by zone, see exact locations on a map, and book instantly. Studio owners get a real dashboard to manage listings and approve bookings. No WhatsApp. No phone calls. No paper calendars.

LensSpace launched with its first partner studios in September 2024. Omar's studio was the first one listed. It still is.

How It Happened

The Evening — Early 2024

Koshary and a Big Idea

A conversation at Omar's place about his studio's booking chaos sparks the idea: one platform, all of Cairo's studios. Omar's connections in the field make it real.

March 2024

Research on the Ground

The team visits studios in Maadi, Nasr City, New Cairo, and Zamalek. Every owner confirms the same problem. Every owner wants in.

April – July 2024

Build Sprint

The platform is built from scratch — studio listings, zone-based search, an interactive Cairo map, a full booking flow, and role-based dashboards for users, owners, and admins.

August 2024

First Partner Studios

Five studio owners across Cairo sign on as founding partners. Omar's Zamalek studio is the very first listing.

September 2024

LensSpace Goes Live

LensSpace launches publicly. In the first month, over 200 bookings are made through the platform — zero WhatsApp messages required.
